Laser Lab · aiming with reflections

How the station works

The laser pivots on a fixed pin at P and nothing else moves. A barrier blocks the straight path to the target T, so the only way to hit it is to bounce the beam off a mirror. Set the angle, fire, and measure the miss. The angle you set is your answer — so work it out before you shoot rather than nudging until it lands.

What to record

Your first-shot angle, whether it hit, and the miss distance in centimetres. A hit on the first shot after constructing T′ is the target result — not a hit after six tries.
